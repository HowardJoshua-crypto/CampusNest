##Based on the deeper needs of each user group, this document defines primary features the university-integrated student housing platform will include:

1. Verified Housing Listings
        -Landlord and property verification process to ensure authenticity.(by an admin)
        -Detailed property profiles with photos, descriptions(available rooms,male/female,single/double), rental terms, and amenities.
        -Clear pricing information to promote transparency.
2. Search and Comparison Tools
        -Filters for location, price, type of accommodation, safety rating, and amenities.
        -Side-by-side comparison of multiple listings.
        -Map view showing proximity to campus and safety zones.
3. Reviews and Ratings
        -Student feedback system for landlords and properties.
        -Rating metrics related to safety, cleanliness, landlord responsiveness, and value for money.
        -Moderation to maintain constructive and genuine reviews.
4. Direct Communication Channels
        -Secure messaging between students and landlords.
        -Option for scheduled visits or virtual tours.
        -Support for negotiation and queries within the platform.
5. Safety and Support Features
        -Neighborhood safety insights based on crime data or student reports.
        -Access to tenant rights information and housing guidelines.
        -Emergency contacts and quick-report options for issues like harassment or unsafe conditions.
6. University Dashboard and Analytics
        -Real-time data on student housing patterns and popular areas.
        -Alerts on reported problems or high-risk zones.
        -Tools for welfare offices and security teams to monitor and communicate with students.
7. Landlord Management Tools
        -Listing management and renewal processes.
        -Analytics on listing views and student inquiries.
        -Premium listing options for better visibility.
8. Payment and Booking Integration (Optional/Future)
        -Secure payment gateways for booking deposits or rents.
        -Booking confirmations and contract management.
        -Service fee options for platform sustainability.
9. User Support and Guidance
        -Help center with FAQs, tenant rights, and dispute resolution advice.
        -Access to on-campus welfare and housing support contacts.
        -Educational content on renting processes and safety tips.

###Sample user experience
1. For Students
        1.Sign Up / Log In
            Register using university credentials for verification.
            Complete profile (optional preferences like budget, room type).
        
        2. Browse and Search Housing Options
            Use filters (location, price, safety rating, amenities).
            View listings with photos, detailed info, and ratings.
            
        3. Compare and Shortlist
            Select favorite listings for easy comparison side-by-side.
            Save or bookmark options.
            
        4. Check Reviews and Safety Info
            Read other students’ feedback and safety scores.
            Access neighborhood safety insights.
            
        5.Communicate with Landlords
            Send inquiries or schedule visits via secure messaging.
            Ask questions or negotiate terms.
        
        6.Confirm Booking (Optional Future Step)
            Pay deposits or booking fees securely if supported.
            Receive confirmation and contract details.
            
        7. Access Support and Resources
            View tenant rights, FAQs, and contact welfare offices.
            Report any issues directly through the platform.
2. For Landlords
        1.  Sign Up / Verification
            Register and provide property documents for verification.
            receive approval status whether approved, pending or declined.
            Get approval from platform moderators.

        2.  Create and Manage Listings
            Upload property details, photos, pricing, and terms.
            Update availability and respond to inquiries.
            
        3.  Interact with Students
            Receive messages and schedule viewings through the platform.
            Maintain communication history.
            
        4.  Track Engagement
            View analytics on listing views, inquiries, and ratings.
            Respond to reviews to build reputation.
            
        5.Upgrade Listings (Optional)
            Opt for premium placements or featured listings for visibility.
3. For University Administrators and Welfare Teams
        1. Access Dashboard
            Log in to view housing data and student residential patterns.
            Filter data by area, housing type, or risk factors.
        
        2. Monitor Safety and Welfare Indicators
            Receive alerts on reported issues or risky neighborhoods.
            Analyze trends to inform policies.

        3. Engage with Students
            Send announcements, safety tips, or emergency alerts via platform.
            Coordinate with security and welfare teams effectively.
        
        4. Support Interventions
            Identify students needing help or housing support.
            Facilitate resolution or connect with landlords.
