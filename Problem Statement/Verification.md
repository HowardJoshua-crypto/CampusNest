##1. Landlord Verification
        -- Identity Verification:
           Require landlords to upload official ID documents (e.g., government ID, passport) and possibly match against university or government databases.
        -- Proof of Ownership:
           Request documents proving ownership or legal rights to rent the property, such as property deeds, rental agreements, or tax documents.
        -- Background Checks:
           Optionally perform background checks or cross-reference with public records to ensure legitimacy.
        -- Interview or Video Call:
            Conduct a short interview or video verification session to confirm identity and commitment.
        --University Partnership:
        Collaborate with the university or local housing authorities to validate landlords with known good records.
##2. Listing Verification
        --Document Submission:
          Require landlords to provide proof of property, such as tax bills, ownership certificates, or rental permits.
        --Photo and Video Verification:
          Require multiple, recent photos or videos of the property interiors, exteriors, and key amenities.
        --On-site Inspection:
          Partner with university welfare or student volunteers to conduct periodic physical inspections (optional but highly effective).
        --Student Feedback:
          Encourage student tenants to submit reviews and report discrepancies between the listing and actual conditions.
        --Automated Checks:
          Use AI or manual review to detect suspicious listings, duplicated properties, or inconsistencies.
##3. Continuous Monitoring
        --Regular Updates:
            Require landlords to periodically renew verification documents to keep listings active.
        --Report and Flag System:
            Allow students to report fraudulent or problematic landlords/listings promptly.
        --Moderation Team:
            Maintain a dedicated team to review reports and take action such as suspending suspicious accounts.
##4. Transparent Verification Badges
        Display verification badges on landlord profiles and listings to show verified status clearly to students.
