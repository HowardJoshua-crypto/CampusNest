###This document provides a deep understanding of the problem we are trying to solve to explain why we need a digital platform addresssing verification, transparency and communication between all parties involved


##Problems Faced by Students
--Lack of Reliable Information: Students often rely on informal networks or unverified sources, leading to misinformation.
--Exposure to Fraud: Fake listings, dishonest brokers, and scams may cause financial loss or hardships.
--High Costs: Many students pay inflated rents due to lack of price transparency and competition.
--Poor Living Conditions: Unsanitary, overcrowded, or unsafe housing options affect students’ health and well-being.
--Safety Concerns: Housing in unsafe neighborhoods or buildings leads to worries about personal security.
--Limited Access to Support: Difficulty reaching trusted contacts or authorities when issues arise.

##Student needs
-- Trustworthy Information: Reliable, verified housing listings with clear details.
-- Affordability: Access to options within budget and transparency on pricing.
--Safety Assurance: Confidence in the security and neighborhood safety of housing.
--Ease of Access: Simple, centralized platform to search, compare, and contact landlords.
--Support and Guidance: Clear advice on tenant rights, contracts, and reporting issues.
--Community Feedback: Reviews or ratings from other students to gauge housing quality.
--Accommodation Variety: Options suited to different needs like shared rooms, private rooms, or family   housing (for mature students).

##Challenges for Universities
--No Centralized Data: Universities do not have systematic insight into where their students live or the conditions of those accommodations.
--Welfare Planning Difficulty: Without data, it is hard to plan support services, emergency responses, or housing policies effectively.
--Safety and Security Risks: Lack of visibility on student housing increases vulnerability in safety incidents.
--Communication Gaps: Difficult to relay important housing-related information or warnings to off-campus students.

##University Administrators needs
--Comprehensive Data: Real-time insights into student residential patterns and conditions.
--Student Welfare Support: Ability to identify housing-related challenges and intervene early.
--Policy Development: Evidence-based data to craft housing guidelines, safety norms, and campus planning.
--Emergency Preparedness: Knowledge of where students live for effective crisis management.
--Communication Channels: Platform to send updates, warnings, and resources directly to students.

##Problems for Landlords and Welfare Units
-- Lack of Transparency: Trusted landlords struggle to reach the right tenants, and there is no easy way to prove credibility.
-- Inefficient Coordination: Student welfare and security units lack streamlined channels to address housing-related issues.

## Landlords needs
--Trust and Credibility: Platform to showcase verified identity and property legitimacy.
--Direct Access to Tenants: Efficient way to reach genuine student renters without middlemen.
--Reputation Building: Positive ratings and feedback to attract more tenants.
--Simplified Management: Tools to manage listings, communicate, and track inquiries.
--Fair Competition: Transparent pricing and fair policies to maintain tenant trust.
