# Business Statement

## Problem Statement
University students, especially freshers, face major challenges in finding safe, affordable, and trustworthy accommodation near campus. Many fall victim to brokers, fake listings, inflated prices, poor sanitation, and unsafe neighborhoods. Universities also lack structured insight into where students stay off-campus, which makes policy development, welfare planning, and safety coordination more difficult.

## Proposed Solution
The proposed solution is a university-integrated digital student housing platform that can be deployed as a section under the official university website. It will help students access verified off-campus housing information, compare accommodation options, evaluate safety and affordability, and connect directly with trusted landlords. At the same time, it will provide university administrators with residential pattern insights that support planning, welfare decisions, and housing policy development.

## Value Proposition
The platform reduces exploitation, improves transparency, and gives students confidence when choosing accommodation. It also equips the university with structured housing intelligence for welfare support, emergency planning, and informed policy action.

## Target Users
- Students
- Landlords
- University administrators
- Student welfare offices
- Security and planning units

## Revenue / Sustainability
- Premium listing fees for landlords
- Featured placement fees
- University analytics subscription or institutional support budget
- Optional booking or service fees at later stages
